Progressive Overload Rule

Wk 1Learn each movement. Log every weight and rep — this is your baseline, not a test.
Wk 2Add 1-2 reps to every set. If last week's top set hit the rep ceiling easily, add +1kg per dumbbell instead.
Wk 3Reps maxed at range (e.g. 4×12)? Bump weight +2-2.5kg and reset to the bottom of the rep range.
Wk 4Repeat the pattern. Target: heavier than Week 1 on every single lift by the end of the block.
